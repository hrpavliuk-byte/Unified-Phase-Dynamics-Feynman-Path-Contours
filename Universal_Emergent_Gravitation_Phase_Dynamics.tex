\begin{abstract}
We construct a unified, non-perturbative theoretical framework demonstrating that macroscopic gravitation emerges deductively from localized quantum phase oscillations across arbitrary energy sectors. Within the \textit{Unified Phase Dynamics of Feynman Path Contours}, rest mass is not an intrinsic fundamental substance, but the total coherent frequency $\omega_{\text{total}} = E_{\text{total}}/\hbar$ of localized quantum phase circulation. While non-perturbative center $\mathbb{Z}_3$ vortex condensation in $SU(3)_c$ generates the analytical spectral mass gap $\Delta_{0^{++}} = 2\sqrt{\pi}\sqrt{\sigma} \approx 1.59\text{ GeV}$ on flat spacetime $\mathbb{R}^4$ in the $G \to 0$ limit (accounting for over $99\%$ of baryonic rest mass), the gravitational phase-generating mechanism is proven to be strictly universal: it applies equally to electromagnetic radiation confined in cavities ($m_0 \equiv 0$), electroweak Higgs masses, kinetic-thermal vibrations, and nuclear binding energies via the unified canonical 4-momentum operator $\mathbf{\Pi}_\mu = p_\mu \mathbf{I} + \mathbf{\mathcal{A}}_\mu$. Every localized packet of energy $E_{\text{total}}$ acts as an intrinsic Compton phase oscillator $\omega_{\text{total}} = E_{\text{total}}/\hbar$, creating a radial phase-density gradient $\Phi(\mathbf{r}) = -G E_{\text{total}} / (c^2 r) = -G M_{\text{eff}} / r$ in the surrounding vacuum master-functional substrate $\mathbf{\Psi}[\Sigma]$. Moving matter wavepackets undergo coherent optical de Broglie refraction, strictly yielding Newton's gravitational law $\mathbf{F} = -G\frac{M m}{r^2}\hat{\mathbf{r}}$ and the Weak Equivalence Principle ($m_{\text{in}} \equiv m_{\text{grav}} \equiv \mathcal{M}_0$). Physical gravity is not mediated by unobservable spin-2 gravitons (Dyson 2013); tensor metric waves propagate at luminal speed $c_T \equiv c$ (GW170817).
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction}
Modern physics is divided by a fundamental barrier: over $99\%$ of baryonic mass originates non-perturbatively from quantum chromodynamics, yet General Relativity couples universally to all forms of energy through $T_{\mu\nu}$. Photons trapped in a mirrored box, thermal kinetic motion, and nuclear binding energies all gravitate identically per unit of energy $E/c^2$, regardless of whether rest mass $m_0$ is present. Conversely, perturbative quantum gravity based on massless spin-2 gravitons is non-renormalizable and operationally unobservable (Freeman Dyson 2013). We resolve this dichotomy by showing that mass is localized quantum phase oscillation, and gravity is the macroscopic optical refraction of de Broglie waves in the resulting phase gradient.
