## Overview
This expanded paper evaluates the anomalous magnetic moments across the charged lepton series ($e, \mu, \tau$) within **Unified Phase Dynamics (UPD)** of Feynman path contours and confronts them with the final **Fermilab 2025** experimental world average ($a_\mu^{\mathrm{Exp}} = 116,592,0715(145) \times 10^{-12}$, $124\ \mathrm{ppb}$).

## Key Quantitative Results
- **Quartic Non-Local Schwinger Form-Factor Suppression ($\Lambda_{\mathrm{UPD}} = 1.2\ \mathrm{TeV}$):**
  - Electron ($e$): $|\delta a_e^{\mathcal{E}}| \approx 3.82 \times 10^{-29}$
  - Muon ($\mu$): $|\delta a_\mu^{\mathcal{E}}| \approx 6.98 \times 10^{-20}$ ($4.81 \times 10^{-10}$ of Fermilab 2025 uncertainty)
  - Tau ($\tau$): $|\delta a_\tau^{\mathcal{E}}| \approx 5.58 \times 10^{-15}$ (enhanced by $(m_\tau/m_\mu)^4 \approx 79,983\times$)
- **Non-Perturbative HVP Alignment:** UPD's $SU(3)_c$ center-vortex confinement scale ($\Delta_{\mathrm{YM}} \approx 1.55\text{--}1.65\ \mathrm{GeV}$) aligns with first-principles Lattice QCD (BMW/Mainz/RBC) HVP determinations, requiring no speculative BSM particles.
