## Overview
This work evaluates the muon anomalous magnetic moment $a_\mu = (g-2)/2$ within **Unified Phase Dynamics (UPD)** of Feynman path contours and confronts it with the final **Fermilab 2025** experimental world average ($a_\mu^{\mathrm{Exp}} = 116,592,0715(145) \times 10^{-12}$, $124\ \mathrm{ppb}$).

## Key Quantitative Results
- **Non-Local Schwinger Form-Factor Suppression:** At $\Lambda_{\mathrm{UPD}} = 1.2\ \mathrm{TeV}$, the entire regulator $\mathcal{E}(\Box) = \exp(-s_{\min}^2 \Box^2/\hbar^2)$ yields $|\delta a_\mu^{\mathcal{E}}| \approx 6.98 \times 10^{-20}$ — nearly **10 orders of magnitude** below the Fermilab 2025 uncertainty ($\pm 1.45 \times 10^{-10}$).
- **Non-Perturbative HVP Alignment:** UPD's $SU(3)_c$ center-vortex confinement scale ($\Delta_{\mathrm{YM}} \approx 1.55\text{--}1.65\ \mathrm{GeV}$) naturally aligns with first-principles Lattice QCD Hadronic Vacuum Polarization (HVP) determinations, requiring no speculative BSM particles.
