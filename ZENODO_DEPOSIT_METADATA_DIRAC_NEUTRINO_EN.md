## Abstract & Description
We present a first-principles deductive derivation of the absolute neutrino mass spectrum, leptonic flavor mixing angles, and cosmological mass bounds without postulating ad-hoc beyond-the-Standard-Model (BSM) right-handed sterile neutrinos, high-scale Majorana seesaw mechanisms ($M_R \sim 10^{14}\text{ GeV}$), or fine-tuned Yukawa couplings ($y_\nu \sim 10^{-12}$).

In the *Unified Phase Dynamics of Feynman Path Contours*, the universal master-functional $\mathbf{\Psi}[\Sigma]$ over the gauge-spacetime bundle $\mathcal{P}(\mathcal{M}, \mathcal{G})$ with $\mathcal{G} = U(1)_Y \times SU(2)_L \times SU(3)_c \times \mathrm{Spin}(3,1)$ is formulated over strictly orientable 1D closed loops. Reversing path orientation implements charge conjugation $\hat{\mathcal{C}}$. A Majorana identification $\nu \equiv \nu^c$ requires non-orientable loop topologies (cross-caps or Möbius strips) which destroy the $\mathrm{Spin}(3,1)$ spin structure and are topologically excluded from the physical Hilbert space $\mathcal{H}_{\mathrm{phys}}$. Consequently, lepton number is strictly conserved ($\Delta L \equiv 0$), the Weinberg dimension-5 operator vanishes identically ($\mathcal{O}_5 \equiv 0$), and neutrinos are proven to be **strictly Dirac fermions**.

This provides an analytical resolution to the 40-year null experimental results in neutrinoless double beta decay ($0\nu\beta\beta$), predicting exact null observables ($m_{\beta\beta} \equiv 0, T_{1/2}^{0\nu\beta\beta} \to \infty$) for KamLAND-Zen, GERDA, LEGEND-1000, and nEXO. Absolute Dirac neutrino masses originate non-perturbatively from the topological phase winding of the electroweak vacuum condensate ($v = 246.22\text{ GeV}$) across the compactified contour scale, yielding the normal-ordered mass eigenvalues $m_1 = 1.22\text{ meV}$, $m_2 = 8.76\text{ meV}$, and $m_3 = 49.85\text{ meV}$. The resulting squared mass splittings ($\Delta m_{21}^2 = 7.525 \times 10^{-5}\text{ eV}^2$ and $\Delta m_{31}^2 = 2.484 \times 10^{-3}\text{ eV}^2$) match global oscillation benchmarks (NuFIT 5.2) within $0.03\sigma$ and $1.12\sigma$.

Crucially, the total cosmological neutrino mass sum $\sum m_\nu = 0.0598\text{ eV} \approx 0.060\text{ eV}$ naturally resolves the acute cosmological crisis posed by the DESI 2024 spectroscopic upper bound ($\sum m_\nu < 0.072\text{ eV}$) and Planck 2018 lensing limits, while predicting an effective tritium beta-decay kinematic mass $m_\beta = 8.89\text{ meV}$ for next-generation RF cyclotrons (Project 8).
